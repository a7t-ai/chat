# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the **Contributor Covenant**, version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. We are
committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, and we expect all
participants in our community, including in issues, pull requests, and any other project space, to
uphold it.

Please read the full text here:

**https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/**

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the
project maintainers at **conduct@a7t.chat** (placeholder, replace with the maintainer's real contact).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and reporter confidentiality is
respected.
